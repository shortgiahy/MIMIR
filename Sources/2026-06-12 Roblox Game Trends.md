## Bottom Line

**Build a RuneScape-style skill-progression grinder. Do not build a full MMO, and do not chase the brainrot/meme wave.**

The shape: one compact co-op world ("skill island"), 4–6 grindable skills (mine/fish/chop/smith/cook/fight), XP curves with visible levels, rebirth/prestige multipliers, AFK-tolerant progress, trading later, weekly content drops from day one. RuneScape-like leveling — exactly the Inbox idea — maps perfectly onto what the platform now rewards.

Why this wins on 2026 fundamentals:
1. **The audience is aging up and Roblox is paying for it.** 18+ users monetize 40–50% higher, the US 18–34 cohort is the fastest-growing (+50% YoY), and since **June 8, 2026** age-verified US 18+ spend pays devs **$0.0054/Robux — a 42% premium**. Roblox is explicitly steering creators toward RPGs/shooters/sports for older players. A nostalgia-grinder targets the exact cohort the platform is subsidizing.
2. **Discovery now rewards retention, not viral CCU.** The Recommended-for-You algorithm weights 24–48h return rate. Skill grinds are return-rate machines — "one more level" is the whole genre.
3. **The meme waves are decaying and clone-saturated.** Even Steal a Brainrot shed ~30% of monthly visits Aug→Dec 2025; late clones launch into a shrinking pool. Solo devs entering that lane now are exit liquidity.
4. **Deep-progression games are a proven niche with a real monetization fallback** (Deepwoken has sold 400-Robux paid access since Dec 2021; fiat paid access now pays 50–70%).
5. **It's solo-buildable with the AI swarm** — agentic Studio Assistant, official MCP server (Claude/Cursor can drive Studio), Cube 3D/CubePart text-to-mesh — *if* scope stays ruthless. Every Roblox MMO that shipped (Vesteria, Rogue Lineage, Deepwoken) took a multi-person team years. The genre is viable; the full-MMO scope is the trap.

**Realistic expectations (read twice):** the median first game earns ~$0. This genre's realistic success case is a mid-tier niche business — roughly $1/player on paid access after Roblox's cut, so 10k–50k lifetime sales ≈ $10k–50k, more with strong pass/cosmetic attach — not Grow a Garden's $12M/month lottery. Treat v1 as the at-bat that teaches you the platform, not the payday.

## 4. What Fails (most things)

- **Winner-take-most economy.** ~$1.5B paid out in 2025, but the top 1,000 creators (avg ~$1.3M) absorb roughly 70%+ of it; only ~100 devs cleared $1M. Millions of experiences split the scraps; the median game earns ~$0.
- **Discovery is the killer.** New games live or die by an opaque recommendation algorithm; devs with top-10%-engagement titles report near-zero visits, and early low-retention playtest sessions can permanently poison a game's ranking (publish-test privately, launch clean).
- **Paid ads don't save you.** ~$0.20–1.00 per engaged player, no real ROI metrics; pointless under roughly 15% D1 retention and 3% icon CTR. Small budgets evaporate.
- **Late clones fail.** The category king itself decayed ~30% in monthly visits Aug→Dec 2025; the Nth "Steal a X" launches into a dying cycle.
- **Slow onboarding, no update cadence, overscope** — the classic three. Top games ship weekly; a 5-minute confusing first session is fatal.
- **Platform risk (2026):** devs keep ~25–28% of ordinary Robux spend (24.5% historic average; Sept 2025 DevEx bump to $0.0038 helped ~8.5%); new pay-to-publish rules for under-16-playable experiences (Roblox Plus sub, shifting to a 1,000-Robux per-game fee from June 2026); Video Stars exodus and policy turbulence. Plan around rules changing under you.

## 5. The RuneScape Angle — precedent and trap

- **Deepwoken** (Monad Studios): hardcore permadeath progression RPG, 400-Robux paid access since Dec 2021, still selling in 2026. Proof that deep progression sustains a paid niche for years. But: built by a team rooted in ~7 people (Rogue Lineage lineage).
- **Arcane Odyssey** (Vetex): one persistent dev, free-to-play progression RPG, alive for years — proof a near-solo progression game can hold an audience. Also proof it takes *years*.
- **Vesteria**: Roblox's flagship explicit MMORPG — 4-person team out of Roblox's incubator. Players paid for MMO depth, but it never touched simulator-tier revenue.
- Pattern: **every notable Roblox progression-MMO shipped with a team and a multi-year build, and none hit simulator money.** The niche monetizes better *per player* (an upfront ~$5 beats the pennies most F2P kids spend) but trades away the viral ceiling.
- Conclusion: keep the *leveling psychology* of RuneScape (XP curves, milestone unlocks, "one more level," visible mastery), drop the *MMO scope* (no open world, no quest webs, no player economy at launch). That's the version one person plus an AI swarm can actually ship.

**AI leverage (the new 2026 variable):** Cube 3D (open-sourced text-to-mesh) and CubePart (May 2026, part-controllable generation, drops rigged assets into the engine), agentic Studio Assistant (plans, executes, self-tests), and an **official Roblox MCP server — Claude/Cursor can drive Studio directly**. This compresses asset + boilerplate time massively, though no chart-topping game is yet publicly credited as AI-built. The swarm idea is current-meta, not fantasy.

## 6. The Money Math

| Channel | Dev take | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary Robux spend (passes/products) | ~25–28% of player $ | $0.0038/Robux DevEx since Sept 5, 2025 |
| Age-verified **US 18+** spend | ~35–38% effective | **$0.0054/Robux since June 8, 2026** — the platform's thumb on the scale for older-audience games |
| Fiat paid access | **50% / 60% / 70%** | $9.99 / $29.99 / $49.99 tiers, since Jan 2025 |
| Rewarded video ads | varies | GA for all eligible creators Feb 2026; bonus on engaged AFK-adjacent audiences |

- Paid-access math at 400 Robux (Deepwoken price): dev nets ~280 R$ × $0.0038 ≈ **~$1.06/sale** (≈$1.51 on US-18+ spend). 10k sales ≈ $10k; 50k ≈ $50k. Fiat $9.99 tier nets $4.99/sale — better per head once there's trust.
- Base rate honesty: typical first game from an unknown dev ≈ $0 (DevForum consensus). The breakouts (GaG $12M in May 2025; SaB est. $11M/mo) are lottery outcomes — and both sold ~50% to operator studios (Splitting Point/Do Big) to scale.

## 7. Recommended Play (concrete, v1)

1. **Scope:** one island, 4–6 skills, co-op servers (8–12 players), F2P launch. No open world. No trading at launch (add it as the season-2 retention hook).
2. **Design for the algorithm:** first 60 seconds = tool in hand, number going up. Session loop 10–20 min; offline/AFK progress so returning always feels like a win (24–48h return rate is the ranking input).
3. **Live-ops from week one:** weekly drop (new resource tier, limited cosmetic, weekend XP event). The cadence *is* the retention system.
4. **Monetize older players:** cosmetics + convenience passes (regional pricing on), rewarded ads at natural AFK points. Aim the theme/aesthetic at 13–17/18+ nostalgia (the $0.0054 cohort), not at under-13s — which also dodges the under-16 publishing fee regime.
5. **Build with the swarm:** Claude/Cursor + Roblox MCP + Rojo for code; Cube/CubePart for first-pass assets; Assistant for in-Studio iteration.
6. **Expectations:** v1 is a reps-and-learning shot. Success = shipped, retained a small cohort, learned the live-ops muscle. Money target for a *good* outcome: $1k–10k in year one; Deepwoken-tier ($50k+/yr niche) is the multi-year ceiling; anything more is luck.
