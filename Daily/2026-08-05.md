## Journal

I'm 21 and I feel 18. Six years of this now, since covid, and I still can't point to what I've actually done with them.

The pattern is clear enough that I can describe it, which somehow makes it worse. I avoid whatever brings dread. The avoidance gives me relief, so I do it again. When things improve slightly, I stop doing whatever produced the improvement, so I slide back. Then I promise something oversized to whoever I've let down, because the promise ends the conversation and buys a few days. Then the date arrives and I've broken it again. Four times now with Natalie's birthday. Thousands of times total, if I'm counting honestly.

I've stopped feeling the anxiety of letting people down. That used to bother me. Now it doesn't, and I've been treating that as toughness. I don't think it is.

What I actually feel is buried. $10k in credit card debt. $218 owed to Natalie by Friday that I don't have. Three finals tomorrow that decide whether I keep the 3.9. Tuition in September. A project I promised a friend that I haven't touched or even updated her about, because I'm embarrassed. Sleeping 14 hours and waking tired. Barely eating. Five days off sertraline.

I keep asking for a system that works without me having to be able to start things. That system doesn't exist. What I have instead is a call to make and three exams to take, and a conversation with Natalie where I say the real number out loud before she finds out on her own.

I'm afraid she leaves. I'm more afraid she stays, tells me it's okay, and I take that relief and disappear again for two weeks. That fear is the honest one. It's the pattern, and I recognized it, and recognizing it is the only thing I've done differently.
