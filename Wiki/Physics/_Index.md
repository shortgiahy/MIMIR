# Physics 1 — Wiki Index

> **When to use this wiki vs. the Math wiki:**
> Use the **Physics wiki** when the question is *physical* — you want to understand what forces, energies, or motions are doing in the real world, or you're solving a Physics 1 problem. Use the **[[Math/_Index|Math wiki]]** when the question is about the *mathematical technique* itself — how to differentiate, integrate, or apply a series. There is intentional overlap: the Physics entries use calculus and explain *why* it appears, but they don't teach calculus. If you hit a math step you can't follow in a Physics entry, go to the Math wiki for the technique, then come back.

## How to Use This Wiki

**If you're starting a topic for the first time:** Read the full entry — don't skip the "Why It Exists" section. The motivation is not filler; it is the fastest way to remember the formula months later when you've forgotten the details.

**If you're stuck on a problem:** Go directly to "Worked Example" and "Gotchas." Read the Gotcha that matches your confusion, then trace back to "The Concept" for the fix.

**If you're reviewing before an exam:** Read "Key Formula / Rule" and "Intuition" for each entry in a cluster. Then attempt the Worked Example from scratch without looking at it.

**If you're lost on the math in a Physics entry:** The Physics entries explain the *why* of every calculus step, but they don't teach calculus. Go to the Math wiki for the technique, come back for the physics application.
