Project Summary: Portable Heated Lotion Belt1. Heating Element & DimensionsBottle Size: 2-inch diameter by 5.5-inch height cylinder (approx. 35 sq. in. surface area).Wire Selection: 5.5 feet of 28 AWG Nichrome 80 wire (approx. 24 Ohms total resistance).Thermal Performance: Lotion heats slowly from the outside inward. It takes 40 to 45 minutes for the absolute center core of a 2-inch bottle to reach 110°F. Shaking the bottle reduces this to under 15 minutes.2. Electrical & Power SystemPower Supply: A compact 12V USB-C PD power bank paired with a 12V decoy trigger cable (or a flat 12V Li-Po battery pack).Power Output: Draws 0.5 Amps and generates 6 Watts of gentle, continuous heat.Efficiency Strategy: A closed-loop control system (such as an ATTiny85 microcontroller or an analog op-amp circuit) cycles power dynamically to maintain a flat 110°F. Because power is only "on" 20% to 30% of the time, an 8-hour shift requires only a 48 Watt-hour battery capacity, drastically reducing battery size and weight.3. Ergonomic & Physical ConstructionThe Layering (Inside to Outside): Aluminum bottle (or HDPE plastic bottle) ➔ Heat-spreading aluminum foil tape ➔ 28 AWG Nichrome wire ➔ Heat-resistant Kapton tape ➔ 3mm to 5mm perforated neoprene belt shell.Component Housing: To maximize comfort and flexibility, components are distributed horizontally around the waist. The logic board uses a coin-sized Rigid-Flex PCB housed inside a flexible, rubbery 3D-printed TPU enclosure to bend naturally with the body.
